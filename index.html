Our story

Born from lived experience

A Different Mind began with founder Elizabeth Stormont, who has lived with mental illness. She knows how deeply a harmful portrayal can hurt, and how powerfully an accurate one can lift people up.

Her story is hers, but it isn’t unique. That’s why we use the arts and popular culture to make room for every kind of mind.

Stigma today

Words shape how we see each other

Stigma is one of the biggest barriers to getting care. Small shifts in everyday language make it easier for people to speak up.

Try thisNot this
Person living with schizophrenia “A schizophrenic”
Died by suicide “Committed suicide”
Has a mental health condition “Crazy” or “insane”
Lives with obsessive-compulsive disorder “I’m so OCD about it”
